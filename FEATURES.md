# Features

## Available now

* **Devices (plotters):** a list on the start screen (add, edit, duplicate, export and import as files, delete), kept between runs. Sheet size (work area), machine zero (any corner), the speed of the moves with the pen up, how the pen is lifted (Z axis or a servo with `M3 S…`) with the up and down values and a wait, notes, and up to 8 cameras (an address or a camera of the computer) with a live Camera tab. **I have no device** makes the Dummy; **New** and **Open…** without a device use it.
* **Pens as layers:** 64 layers with a colour, a pen width, a speed, outline / fill with lines / both, the angle and gap of the fill lines, crossed lines, and how many times every line is drawn. Layers can be switched off (not drawn) and hidden (not shown).
* **The plotter:** a GRBL controller on a serial port or a network socket (set in the device: port, baud rate, the GRBL version (1.1 or newer, 1.0, 0.9, 0.8 or 0.7 or older), streaming, homing or clearing an alarm after connecting); Connect, Home, Unlock, Pause / Resume, the state and position, a **Console** tab (the traffic with the controller, a line to send a command); Raise the pen / Lower the pen (where it stands, by the Z axis or the servo); Frame (the pen goes round the box of the drawing, lifted), Start and Stop of the job and Save G-code on the control bar. The Toolpaths button draws the paths over the work area; Camera view shows the live camera.
* **Plot and G-code:** strokes in the order that shortens the moves with the pen up, fill lines that zigzag and leave holes empty, a preview window (zoom, pan, an animation with play, pause, speed and repeat, length, pen lifts, time) and G-code for GRBL (`M0` to change the pen between layers, the machine zero by corner). Pictures cannot be drawn and are reported.
* **Tabs:** every open drawing has a tab above the work area (File → New and Open make one, up to 10; a project that is open is shown, a blank tab is used as it is). A dot marks a tab with unsaved work, the cross closes it (asking Save, Discard or Cancel when there is unsaved work), a middle click closes it too. Every tab with unsaved work has its own recovery file, and after a crash the start screen offers to recover them all, each in a tab. Closing the program asks about each tab with unsaved work in turn: the tab is shown, you answer, it is closed, the next one follows; Cancel stops the closing. Ctrl+1 … Ctrl+9 and Ctrl+0 show the tabs, Ctrl+W closes the shown one (the last tab closes the program).
* **Pen change at a fixed place** (device settings, page Pen): a place in the coordinates of the work area (it may lie outside), optional G-code for a mechanism and a switch for the stop (`M0`); the plotter lifts the pen, drives there, runs the lines and stops when the next layer needs another pen. Without a place the job stops where it is.
* **Job options** (in the Control tab, under Frame and From line…; this session only, together or alone): **One element at a time** draws each element (a group, even over several layers, or a shape that is in no group) completely, with all its layers and passes, before the next one, the nearest first (meant for layers with the same pen); **Selection only** draws just the shapes that are selected when the G-code is saved or the job is started. Both change the G-code, the preview, the estimate and the frame.
* **Device and Control tabs:** under the connection and the position (a line between), Home, Unlock, Pause and Resume are a block of 2 × 2 with icons (Home blue, Unlock red, Pause orange, Resume green); the same block is at the top of the Control tab, above the position, and under them a wide STOP button that is live only while the machine works. The Zero buttons are a grid of 2 × 2, and the sections run: position, zero, Move, the jog buttons, Speed.
* **Project file:** `.cre`, shared with LightCreator and MillCreator: the drawing, the description, the `pen` section (layers and the plotter it was made for, with a warning when it is opened for another sheet size) and the sections of other programs, which are kept as they were.
* The window of LightCreator's look: own frame and title bar, start screen, control bar, status bar, a Properties tab, the clipart gallery (310 included pictures, your own, online search), a Description tab, the colour schemes, fonts and 30 languages (PenCreator's own texts too).
* Reading SVG, PDF, Illustrator (PDF-compatible) and **DXF** (ASCII R12 and newer: lines, polylines with bulges, arcs, circles, ellipses, splines, blocks, layers, the unit of the file; lines that meet are joined) files and LightBurn projects (`.lbrn2`: shapes, text outlines, bitmaps) and UGS Designer designs (`.ugsd`: pockets are filled, the rest is outlined) into a scene of shapes.
* **Pictures:** File → Import → Import image… (or drop a PNG, JPG, BMP, GIF or WebP file on the window) opens the import dialog (rotate, flip, brightness, contrast, gamma, auto levels, negative, size); Arrange → **Adjust image…** changes a picture on the page, and **Trace image…** turns its dark (or light) areas into outlines (threshold, speckle filter, simplification, curve smoothing) that are cut or drawn like any other shape. The picture itself is only shown. The import and adjust dialog follows the size of the window and has a **Levels** column: a histogram of the greys with two sliders for the range (black and white point). The trace dialog zooms (scroll wheel) and moves (drag) its preview. The tool palette shrinks its buttons when the window is too low for it.
* **Looking for a newer release** on GitHub when the program starts (switch in Settings → Program options…) and by hand in Help → Check for updates…; it only asks which release is newest and opens the download page, nothing is installed by itself.
* **Layer bar** under the editor, the same in all programs: one row of the 64 layer colours (arrows or the wheel scroll it; a dot marks layers that have shapes). Click a colour to put the selected shapes on that layer (or, with nothing selected, to draw on it); double-click for its options.
* **Plugins** (Settings → Plugins…, shared with the other programs on baseCreator): clipart, machine presets, translations, importers of other file formats, shape generators with a form, and G-code postprocessors, as folders or zips; scripts run in a sandbox (Rhai) and the postprocessor output is checked. An **official list** of plugins (all programs' plugins, Install / Update with a checksum, updates checked only when you ask or switch it on) is in Plugins… → Official plugins. Every plugin that has something to set (a postprocessor) has a **tab of its own** in the right-hand panel; the official plugins are translated into Polish, German, Italian, Finnish, Chinese and Hindi.
* The editor of baseCreator: drawing tools, selection, node editing, undo and redo, copy and paste, group, align, flip, rotate, order, grid and snapping.
* Menus (with the native macOS menu bar and recent files), icons, tool palette, arrange bar, View options and the About window of baseCreator.
* **Machine control:** the **Control** tab (work and machine position, zero of X, Y, Z and G54 … G59, jogging by steps or by holding a button, keyboard jogging, go to X0 Y0, macros with variables and G-code before and after every job); the job stops at a change of the pen (a window, continue / stop); **Start from line…**; the program can be checked in the controller's check mode first; the settings of the controller (`$$`) with search, a filter for what differs from typical, writes after a question, an automatic backup before the first change, backup to and restore from a file, factory reset behind a typed word; calibration of steps per millimetre and of the squareness of the axes; diagnostics (input pins, firmware, jobs, running time, alarms, errors, a zip with report, settings, console and program) and maintenance reminders; alarms and errors of GRBL explained in 30 languages; a device can be read from the controller (travel, speed) and started from a usual sheet size; **copies of the settings of the controller** are kept with the device (made at the first connection and whenever the settings differ from the newest copy, with a message listing what changed; by hand, restore and delete from the firmware window; part of the backup of the program). None of it has been tried on a real plotter.
* **Backup:** Help → Download backup… saves a zip with the settings of PenCreator and the shared clipart gallery; Help → Restore from backup… reads it back (settings replaced after a confirmation, pictures added to the gallery, a backup of another program refused). The same entries as in LightCreator.
* **Pictures are drawn:** a picture put on a layer switches the layer to the Picture drawing; the Picture method is dithered rows, dots or hatching by tone, with a choice of the dithering (threshold, Floyd–Steinberg, Jarvis, Stucki, Atkinson, ordered) and the gap and angle of the lines.
* **Pens as tools:** Settings → Pen library… (and the … button next to the layer's Pen choice) is a list of ready-made pens (pencil, fountain pen, fineliner, felt-tip marker, highlighter, brush pen) and your own, with the width and height of the mark, the turn of the tip, the ink colour, the opacity and the ends of a line; they can be copied, exported and imported as files. A layer takes the pen's width, and the preview draws every line as the pen makes it: a chisel tip is thick across and thin along, a highlighter is see-through so overlaps get darker. Layers keep the pen by its id in the project and the pens are saved with it.
* **Right-click menu:** on a shape (copy, paste, duplicate, delete, group, lock, flip, turn, arrange, shape operations: union, intersection, subtract, exclusive or, offset, round corners, convert to curves; adjust and trace a picture), the same in all programs.
