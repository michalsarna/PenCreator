# Design tools

PenCreator edits shapes with the same tools as LightCreator and MillCreator (baseCreator).

* **Right mouse button** on a shape opens the context menu: copy, paste, duplicate, delete, select all, group, ungroup, lock, unlock, flip, turn, the *Arrange* submenu (order, alignment, centring, edit nodes, convert to path) and the *Shape operations* submenu (union, intersection, subtract, exclusive or, offset shape, round corners, convert to curves; they are also in the **Arrange** menu); for a picture also *Adjust image…* and *Trace image…*. The entries that do not fit the selection are grey.
* **DXF:** **File → Import → Import DXF…** (or **File → Open…** and drag and drop) reads an ASCII `.dxf` (R12 and newer): lines, polylines with bulges, arcs, circles, ellipses, splines and blocks as outlines, in millimetres (the unit of the file is read), arcs and circles as curves; lines that meet become one path. The layers of the DXF go to the layers of the project that have no shapes yet, one after the other. What is not a line is left out and named in the status bar. **Arrange → Shape operations → Auto-join paths…** / **Close path with tolerance…** and **File → Export → Export selection as SVG… / DXF…** work as in the other programs.
* **Pictures (bitmaps):** **File → Import → Import image…** (or drop a PNG, JPG, BMP, GIF or WebP file on the window) opens the import dialog: rotate, flip, brightness, contrast, gamma, auto levels, negative and size. **Adjust image…** (in the *Image* section of the Properties tab, or **Arrange → Adjust image…**) changes a picture on the page, and **Trace image…** (the same button, or **Arrange → Trace image…**) turns its dark (or light) areas into outlines (threshold, speckle filter, simplification, curve smoothing) that are drawn like any other shape. A picture put on a layer switches that layer to the **Picture** drawing (once; you can change it back), and then the picture itself is drawn with lines, by the **Picture method** of the layer: **Dithered rows** (the picture is dithered and every row is drawn as lines where it is dark), **Dots** (a dot for every dark cell) or **Hatching by tone** (crossed hatching: the darker the picture, the more directions of lines). For the first two you choose the **Dithering** (threshold, Floyd–Steinberg, Jarvis, Stucki, Atkinson, ordered); the **Fill line gap** and **angle** of the layer set the distance and the direction of the rows. On a layer with another drawing a picture is only shown.
* **Side panel:** drag a tab to put it in another place (the order is remembered, also in a backup). The small arrow at the left of the tabs hides the panel; a narrow strip with the names of the tabs stays, and a click on a name opens the panel on that tab.
* **Esc twice** (a second Esc within half a second) goes back to the Select tool.
* **Layer bar** under the editor: one row of 64 colours (a dot marks the layers with shapes); a click puts the selected shapes on that layer, a double click opens its options.

## Arranging, copying and generating

The **Insert** menu has the tools of the left palette that insert objects (rectangle, ellipse, triangle, star, polygon, heart, spiral, line, polyline / pen and text; the tool in use is ticked), then the generators of plugins and the online clipart.

Commands of the *Arrange* menu, after *Dogbone / T-bone corners…* (the same in LightCreator, MillCreator and PenCreator):

* **Auto-join paths…** joins open paths whose ends are nearer than a tolerance; **Close path with tolerance…** closes the ones whose two ends are that near. **Dogbone / T-bone corners…** adds a notch to every sharp corner so that a round bit reaches it (milling). **Export selection as SVG / DXF…** writes the selected shapes (all when nothing is selected).
* **Circular array…** puts copies round the middle of the selection over a *Total angle* (360° goes all the way round), turned with their place or only moved; the grid array is the one of the Arrange menu. At most 2000 copies are made.
* **Copy along path…**: select the shapes, then the path **last**; copies are put every *Distance* along it and may be turned with it. **Text on path…**: select the text, then the path last; the outlines of the text are bent along the path from *Start* (afterwards they are outlines, not text).
* **Trim by shape…**: select the lines, then a closed shape **last**; the parts inside (or outside) are kept, cut where the lines cross its outline. **Remove duplicates** deletes shapes with the same outline on the same layer (the selection, or the whole drawing). **Break apart** makes one shape of every outline. **Simplify…** takes away the points within a *Tolerance* of the line.
* **Serial text…**: select a text; it is copied in a grid with its `{n}` counted up, or, with **Read a CSV file…**, once for every row, `{1}`, `{2}`… being the columns. **Barcode / QR code…** puts a QR code or a Code 39 barcode on the active layer as outlines (make them large enough for the job).
* **Nest shapes…** packs the selected shapes in rows from the corner of the page on an area you give, with a distance between them (by their boxes, not their outlines; quarter turns can be allowed). **Measure…** shows the size, position, area and length of the selection and, for two shapes, the distances between them.

## Background picture (View menu) and the trace dialog

**View → Background picture…** puts a photo, a plan or a scan **under the drawing**: **Load a picture from a file…** (PNG, JPG, BMP, WebP, GIF). Tick **Move the corners** and drag its four corners onto the corners of the material or of the work area (the picture follows with a perspective transform); **Opacity**, **Show**, **Fit to the work area** and **Remove** are in the same window. The picture is not saved with the project. In the **Trace image** dialog the picture can be zoomed with the scroll wheel or the **zoom in**, **zoom out** and **reset view** buttons, and the green **Trace** button makes the outlines.
