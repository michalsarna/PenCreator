# Safety and Limitations

PenCreator is an **early skeleton**. It is a drawing editor with import and a project file; it **does not make plotting paths, write G-code or HPGL, or connect to a plotter**.

* The work area is fixed (A4, 297 × 210 mm).
* There are no layers with pens, no hatching and no ordering of strokes.
* Texts of the program's own screens are English; the shared ones are in 30 languages.
* What is tested: the editor and the project file (the sections of other programs are kept) and the opening of files, by automatic tests on every change.

When plotting arrives: a plotter has moving parts and pens that can dry out or damage the sheet; test new jobs with the pen lifted and stay at the machine.
