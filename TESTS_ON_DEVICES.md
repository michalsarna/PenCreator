# PenCreator: tests on devices

PenCreator has been tried on these machines and works there. The program is made for many pen plotters and drag-knife cutters; a machine that is not on the list may work just as well, it has only not been confirmed yet.

| Device | Kind | Controller |
|---|---|---|
| Pen plotter built after the [Drawing Robot](https://www.printables.com/model/137296-drawing-robot-arduino-uno-cnc-shield-grbl) | pen plotter | Arduino UNO, CNC Shield, GRBL |

## Add your machine

Does PenCreator run on your plotter? Open an [issue](https://github.com/michalsarna/PenCreator/issues/new) with:

* the make and model of the machine, and what it is (for example a diode laser, a CNC mill, a pen plotter);
* the controller and its firmware with the version (for example GRBL 1.1h);
* the version of PenCreator and the system of the computer (Windows, macOS, Linux);
* what you did with it (a job, framing, jogging, the camera…) and whether something went wrong.

It goes on the list once it is confirmed.
